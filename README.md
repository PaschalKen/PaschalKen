## 🎯 Featured Projects

### [ERP-Style Business Management System](https://github.com/PaschalKen/Seeman-Business-management) — Final Year Project
**88% — highest mark in cohort**

Full-stack business management system built and deployed for an automotive and agro parts distributor: inventory, sales, fulfilment, reporting and staff administration across multiple branches.

Clean Architecture and CQRS, event-driven activity logging, role-based auth, Redis caching, IndexedDB offline support and real-time analytics dashboards — requirements gathered directly from the client and validated through user acceptance testing.

`React` `TypeScript` `Redux` `NestJS` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `Tailwind CSS`

### 🏋️ [Subira](https://github.com/PaschalKen/Subira) — Workout Tracker
Mobile-first PWA for tracking gym sessions, exercises and sets. An ongoing exploration of offline-first architecture and local ↔ server synchronisation.

`Vue 3` `TypeScript` `ASP.NET Core` `C#` `EF Core` `PostgreSQL` `Dexie`

### 🔥 [SeeFit](https://github.com/PaschalKen/SeeFit) — HIIT Training App · 81%, highest in cohort
Interval training app where users build custom workouts from timed exercise and recovery periods, then perform them with a live countdown, progress ring and audio cues.

Installable as a Progressive Web App, with a full create/edit/delete flow, light and dark themes, and a dashboard tracking completed workouts. Designed against Nielsen's usability heuristics, with a REST API over SQLite behind it.

`JavaScript` `Node.js` `Express` `SQLite` `PWA`

### 🐣 [Virtual Pet](https://github.com/PaschalKen/Virtual-Pet) — Interactive Pet Simulator
Browser-based virtual pet with happiness, hunger, cleanliness and sleep meters that decay in real time — feed, clean, play with or wake the pet to keep it alive, and retrieve a saved pet on return visits.

Built with a modular ES module structure and a small JSON-backed persistence layer.

`JavaScript` `Node.js` `HTML` `CSS`
